Dr. Tuan A. Pham is an internationally recognized geotechnical engineering researcher advancing climate-resilient infrastructure through adaptive intelligence, integrating physics-based modeling, artificial intelligence, digital twins, and risk-informed decision-making. He obtained two master's degrees in France and Japan and a PhD in the UK, and worked as a researcher in Sweden. His research contributions have received national and international recognition, including the Canada Impact+ Research Award (2026), Vietnam Outstanding Young Faces Award (2026), Golden Globe Award in Science and Technology (2025), and Rising Star Award in Geotechnical Engineering (2025).
