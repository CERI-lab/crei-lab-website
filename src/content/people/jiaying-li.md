Jiaying Li is a PhD student in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Tufts University, advised by Prof. Farshid Vahedifard and Prof. Deborah Sunter. Her research focuses on the interactions among civil infrastructure, human mobility, and climate hazards across local, regional, and global scales. She combines network analysis, computational social science, and geospatial big data to study how urban and infrastructure systems adapt to and recover from extreme events such as wildfires and floods. Her work connects risk assessment with social vulnerability analysis to support equitable infrastructure planning and climate adaptation, especially for disadvantaged communities. Under a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE)-funded project, she also applies energy capacity expansion models to evaluate renewable energy transitions and power grid integration. Prior to joining Tufts, she received her B.S. in Hydraulic Engineering from Sichuan University and her M.S. in Smart Cities and Big Data from Peking University.
