Mohammed Azhar is a PhD candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Tufts University, working with Professor Farshid Vahedifard. He earned his bachelor’s and master’s degrees from the Asian Institute of Technology in Bangkok, then spent two and a half years as a geotechnical engineer. In that role, he designed shallow and deep foundations, interpreted laboratory soil testing programs, and conducted forensic analyses of dam failures. His research interests have since grown into applying geotechnical ideas to the broader goal of resilient and equitable infrastructure. Toward this effort, he studies how hazards such as flooding, land subsidence, and earthquakes threaten levee systems across the U.S., and how the impacts of levee failure fall disproportionately on vulnerable communities.
