Stefan Flynn is a PhD candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Tufts University and is the Senior Geotechnical Specialist for the USACE Levee Safety Center. He uses historic performance data to model levee overtopping and study levee risk. He earned his MS at Mississippi State University, a BS in Civil Engineering from Southern Illinois University at Edwardsville, and a BA in Physics at Monmouth College (IL). He has received numerous professional awards including the 2024 ASCE Embankments, Dams, and Slopes Younger Member of the Year and the USACE 2021 Early Career Geoprofessional Award. Outside the lab, Stefan spends his time with his wife, Karrah, and daughter, Lucy.
