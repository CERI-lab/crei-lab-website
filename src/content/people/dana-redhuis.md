Dana Redhuis is a PhD student in the lab focused creating community-centric natural hazard mitigation solutions. Her current work examines hazard impacts on housing systems in the U.S. Gulf Coast as part of a multidisciplinary effort to develop a housing resilience decision-support framework. Before joining the lab she worked as a Geospatial Ecologist at Michigan Tech Research Institute, and has a M.S. from University of Michigan in Environmental Conservation Management and Environmental Justice. Her B.S. is in Geography and Environmental Science from Aquinas College. Outside of the lab she enjoys training for marathons and reading.
