Hossein Bazdar is a PhD student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Tufts University. He holds an MSc in Geotechnical Engineering from the University of Tehran and a BSc in Civil Engineering from K. N. Toosi University of Technology. His current research focuses on developing digital twins for levee systems, integrating physics-based modeling, field monitoring, and machine learning. He is particularly interested in advancing hybrid physics-based and data-driven approaches to better understand and predict the behavior of geotechnical systems. Outside the lab, Hossein enjoys exploring new technologies, traveling, cooking, and working out.
